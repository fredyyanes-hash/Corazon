Our Story

Where Family Tradition Meets Authentic Flavor

Corazón Cocina Mexicana is a proudly family-owned restaurant — not a chain, not a franchise — rooted in the genuine traditions of Mexican cooking. Every dish that leaves our kitchen reflects generations of authentic recipes, prepared by Mexican chefs using only the freshest ingredients.

From our hand-rolled corn tamales to our sizzling fajitas, every bite carries the soul of true Mexican cuisine. We've earned our reputation as Orlando's best, and we continue earning it every single day.

The Corazón Promise

Every meal at Corazón is prepared with the love and care of a family kitchen — because that's exactly what it is. We are committed to serving this community for generations to come, carrying forward the tradition that defines the Corazón Family.

No shortcuts. No compromises. Just pure, honest Mexican food the way it was always meant to be made.

The Atmosphere

Step Into a Mexican Hacienda

The moment you enter Corazón, you leave Orlando behind. Our Spanish-influenced architecture wraps you in warmth — rustic open ceiling timbers overhead, hand-painted Talavera tiles beneath your feet, and vibrant murals celebrating Mexican artistry on every wall.

This isn't just a restaurant. It's an experience. A destination. A place where every detail has been crafted to transport you to the heart of Mexico.

  • Hand-painted Talavera tiles and colorful Mexican murals throughout the dining room
  • Rustic open ceiling timbers and Spanish-influenced architectural details
  • Warm, relaxing atmosphere perfect for family dinners and celebrations
  • Outdoor patio dining for those beautiful Orlando evenings
